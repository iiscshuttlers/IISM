# IISc Badminton Training Camp — Courtside Quick Guide

A quick reference for players, coaches, and camp administrators.

---

## 1. Getting Started

### Sign In
1. Open the camp link on your phone or computer.
2. Tap **Continue with Google**.
3. Sign in using the exact email the camp admin added to your profile on the roster.
4. *Phone Tip:* Tap your browser menu (**Share** on iOS Safari or **⋮** on Android Chrome) and select **Add to Home Screen** to use the site as an app.

*If you see "You're not on a camp roster":* Contact the camp admin to make sure your email is correctly attached to your profile in Camp Settings.

---

## 2. Match Recording

### How to Record
1. Go to **Matches** and tap **Record match**.
2. Select the category:
   - **MS / WS**: Men's / Women's Singles (2 players).
   - **MD / WD**: Men's / Women's Doubles (4 players).
   - **XD**: Mixed Doubles (1 male + 1 female per side).
3. Pick the players for Team 1 and Team 2.
4. Pick who won (**Side 1** or **Side 2**).
5. *(Optional but recommended)* Enter the game scores (e.g. `21-17`, `19-21`, `21-18`).
6. Tap **Save match**.

### Rules & Dispute Policy
- **Editing (24 Hours):** The person who recorded the match can edit it within 24 hours while it remains `confirmed`.
- **Disputing (24 Hours):** Any player who played in the match can dispute it within 24 hours if the score or winner was entered incorrectly. Enter a clear reason when disputing.
- **Resolution:** A coach or admin reviews the dispute and can either resolve it with updated scores/winner or void it.
- **Standings:** Only `confirmed` matches affect ratings. Disputed and voided matches don't count until a coach resolves them.

## 3. Training & Daily Effort (RPE)

### For Coaches
- Create a session for the day (e.g. "Morning Session — Attack & Transition").
- Add drills with target duration, sets, reps, and intensity (1–5).
- Mark attendance (**Present**, **Late**, or **Absent**).

### For Players — Rating of Perceived Exertion (RPE)
At the end of every session, players or coaches log an RPE score from **1 to 10** reflecting how hard the session felt physically:

| RPE | Exertion Level | Description |
|:---:|:---|:---|
| **1–2** | Very Light | Barely breaking a sweat, easy warm-up |
| **3–4** | Moderate | Breathing a bit heavier, comfortable to converse |
| **5–6** | Hard | Challenging rally pace, high sweating, speaking in short sentences |
| **7–8** | Very Hard | Near-limit physical push, anaerobic intervals, heavy breathing |
| **9–10** | Maximal Effort | Complete exhaustion, match point pressure, full capacity |

Consistent RPE logging helps coaches monitor fatigue, prevent injuries, and assess dedication.

### Leave and exams
- **Players:** Away for exams, illness or travel? Tell the camp admin before you go. Once it's marked, you'll see it on your Today screen.
- **Admin:** Mark it in **Settings → Leave and exams** (player, first and last day, reason). Only the admin can add, change or remove leave; coaches and the player can see it.
- **What it changes:** Sessions missed on those days don't count against the player's attendance. On Today their name shows **Leave**, they're left out of **All present** and the not-marked count, and the pairing helper skips them. If they turn up anyway, mark them present as usual.

## 4. Standings & Ratings

- **Starting Rating:** Every player begins at **1200**.
- **How it moves:** Each confirmed match moves your rating (the Elo system). Beating a stronger side gains more than beating a weaker one.
- **Settled dot:** Red means too few matches to trust the rating yet; green means it has settled.
- **Doubles Rating:** For doubles pairs, the effective rating used is the average rating of both partners.
- **By discipline:** Standings can switch between Overall, Singles, Doubles and Mixed. Each discipline has its own rating that only moves with that kind of match.
- **Form:** Displays your last 5 match results (e.g. `WWLWW`).
- **Privacy:** Depending on camp settings, players can either see the full leaderboard or their own personal row.

---

## 5. Players & Assessments

- **Players tab (staff):** Every player in one list with rating, wins, attendance, drill minutes, average effort (RPE) and coach score. Sort by any of them, filter by men or women or by what they're training for, and tap a name for the full record.
- **Training for:** On a player's profile, coaches tap **S** (singles), **D** (doubles) and/or **XD** (mixed) — any combination. It shows as S · D · XD under their name in the Players list and next to their name when picking players; when recording a match, the event that match counts for is in green.
- **Finding a player:** Every player picker has a search box that forgives typos — typing "Rsja" still finds Raja, and "rk" finds Raja Kumar.
- **Assessments:** Coaches score players on **Technique**, **Fitness**, **Tactics**, and **Attitude** (1–5 scale) with **Assess**. Players never see these scores.
- **Shortlist (admin only):** **Players → Shortlist** ranks every player by one score out of 100 built from rating, coach score, attendance (leave excluded) and win %. Slide how much each counts and save. Coaches and players can't see the page or the weights. It's a guide for the selection conversation, not the decision.
