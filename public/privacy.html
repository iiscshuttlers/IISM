Privacy policy

IISc Training Camp · last updated 14 November 2026

IISc Training Camp is a private website used by the players and coaches of an IISc badminton selection camp to record practice matches and training, and to support squad selection. It is not open to the public.

What we store

  • Profile: name, gender (used for match categories) and, only if the camp admin adds it, an email address used to sign in.
  • Sign-in with Google: we receive your name, email address and Google account identifier.
  • Sign-in with email and password: we store your email address and a password that is kept only in hashed form by our authentication provider (Supabase); nobody, including the camp admin, can read it. Supabase may send you an email to confirm your address or reset your password.
  • Either way, a sign-in is linked to a profile only after its email address is confirmed and matches the one the camp admin entered.
  • Camp records: practice match results, training sessions, drills, attendance, self-reported effort (RPE), coach assessments and selection decisions.

How it is used

Only to run the camp: showing results and standings, logging training, and helping coaches select the squad. There is no advertising, no tracking or analytics, and no location data.

Who can see it

  • Only people on the camp roster can sign in and see camp data.
  • The camp admin decides whether players see everyone's results or only their own, and when the squad is published.
  • Coach assessments and selection notes are visible to coaches and the camp admin only.
  • We do not sell or share data with anyone. It is not shared with any other app, including the IISc Badminton Club app.

Where it is stored

Data is stored in a Supabase (PostgreSQL) database protected by row-level security. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages.

Keeping and deleting data

Data is kept for the duration of the camp and for the selection record afterwards. You can ask the camp admin to correct or delete your profile and records at any time.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: contact the camp admin through the camp group.
